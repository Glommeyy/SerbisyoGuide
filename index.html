    A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

    Good to know

    Is this an official barangay website?

    No. SerbisyoGuide is an academic demonstration. Its sample requirements need verification with a partner barangay before real use.

    Can I submit documents here?

    This site helps you prepare. Applications, document verification, and payments must follow your barangay’s official process.

    Where is my checklist saved?

    Your latest generated checklist and progress are saved in localStorage on this device and browser when storage is available. No account or database is used.

    How do I save a PDF?

    Generate a checklist, select “Print / Save PDF”, then choose “Save as PDF” in your browser’s print dialog.
